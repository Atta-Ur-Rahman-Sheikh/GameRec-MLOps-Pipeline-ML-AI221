\section{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Static catalogue}: models snapshot a moment in time; seasonal esports spikes are not live data.
\item \textbf{Popularity variance}: Bayesian shrinkage narrows targets, capping $R^2$.
\item \textbf{Tag noise}: community tags include memes, spoilers, and marketing fluff.
\item \textbf{Cold items}: unseen titles rely on textual proxies without collaborative histories.
\item \textbf{Compute}: full TF--IDF + SVD on laptops can take tens of minutes—batch size and sparse dtypes matter.
\end{itemize}
